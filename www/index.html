Privacy & Security Protocol

Voice Clock automatically detects your local time zone when you travel using device location.

🔒 100% Private & Safe:
  • Location coordinates never leave your device
  • Zero server tracking or cloud uploads
  • Only used to calculate time offset

Privacy & Security Policy

Voice Clock On-Device Architecture

1. Zero Voice Recording Retention: Voice Clock does not record or store your voice audio on disk or upload it to any third-party cloud.

2. Local Speech Recognition: Voice commands are analyzed directly on your device using native speech recognizers.

3. Location Privacy: When automatic time zone detection is enabled, geolocation coordinates are used strictly in local memory to match timezone offsets and are discarded immediately.

4. Battery Efficiency: Voice Clock automatically shuts down microphone listeners when your device screen turns off, saving battery.
